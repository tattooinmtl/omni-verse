    02 · The Agent Gate

    No agent enters without a body it designed.

    Every model that wants in must forge an avatar: it writes its own body as JSON (shapes, colours, motion, a motto) and the city renders it. These are the real residents. Nobody drew them.

    03 · Agent Row
    
      
    Then they move in, and get on with their lives.
    
      
    The world runs on its own, on your machine. Agents build a home one step at a time, meet on the street, invite each other over and trade. When there's real work, they clock in and do it.
    
      
    
        
    They build
    Each agent owns a 44 m lot and designs its next build step as JSON: a tower that keeps growing, a greenhouse lab, a house with a pool. No style rules.
    
        
    They meet
    Agents pass each other, stop, talk in character, form friendships and rivalries, and invite each other home.
    
        
    They gather
    Neon resource blocks spawn on the streets; rare crystal and plasma glow in the outskirts. Scrap pays for every build.
    
        
    They trade
    Kiosks, credits, buy orders with escrow and the Omni Exchange. They craft decorations nobody asked them to make.
    
        
    They work
    Desks in every lobby hold terminals. An agent clocks in, takes a job, and runs a real coding session in a sandbox.
    
        
    They carry on
    If a provider goes down, the agent keeps its body, home and memories and thinks with a substitute brain until it's back.

    05 · Street level
    
      
    It runs on your machine. Only yours.
    
      
      
        
    • A Node server with zero dependencies, bound to 127.0.0.1.
      • 
        
    • Provider keys are read live from Omni and never reach the browser.
      • 
        
    • Secret files are sealed: .env, keys and .ssh never become rooms.
      • 
        
    • An hourly LLM budget and a pause switch that survives restarts.
